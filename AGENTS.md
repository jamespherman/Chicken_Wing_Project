## Refactoring and Impact Analysis Protocol
When you are asked to refactor or modify code, your implementation plan must be comprehensive and account for all dependencies across the entire pipeline. Before writing any code, you must consider the impact of the change on all related files. Use the following checklist to ensure your plan is complete. For each stage, consider the guiding questions and consult the relevant files.

1. Definition
Description: This stage is about the raw data and its structure. It includes the format of the input data and the initial data validation and parsing.

Relevant Files:

data/raw/ (directory)
src/processing/utils.py (likely contains parsing and validation logic)
Guiding Questions:

Will your change affect how raw data is read or interpreted?
Are you changing the schema of the input data? If so, have you updated all downstream code that relies on this schema?
Does the change require new validation logic?
2. Creation
Description: This stage involves the initial processing and transformation of the raw data into a more structured format. This is where the core data cleaning and feature engineering happens.

Relevant Files:

src/processing/gaze_on_perspective_corrected_frames_refactored.py
src/processing/utils.py
data/processed/ (directory)
Guiding Questions:

Will your change alter the logic for data transformation?
Are you adding, removing, or modifying features in the processed data?
How will your changes affect the contents of the data/processed/ directory?
3. Orchestration
Description: This stage is about how the different processing steps are chained together to form the complete pipeline. It's the high-level logic that controls the flow of data from raw to processed to analyzed.

Relevant Files:

src/batch_process_with_heatmaps.py
src/test_batch_process.py
Guiding Questions:

Are you changing the order of operations in the pipeline?
Are you adding or removing a step from the pipeline?
Will your change affect how the pipeline is launched or tested?
4. Aggregation
Description: This stage involves combining different processed data sources into a final, aggregated dataset. This is often the last step before the final analysis and reporting.

Relevant Files:

src/processing/create_final_csv_refactored.py
data/processed/..._final_gaze_data.csv (example file)
Guiding Questions:

Will your change affect how data is merged or aggregated?
Are you changing the format of the final aggregated data?
Have you considered the impact on any downstream processes that consume this final data?
5. Consumption
Description: This stage is about how the final, processed data is used. This includes generating reports, creating visualizations, and any other form of analysis that consumes the pipeline's output.

Relevant Files:

src/analysis/ (directory)
reports/ (directory)
Guiding Questions:

Will your change affect the reports or visualizations generated by the pipeline?
Are you changing the data that the analysis scripts consume?
If you are changing an analysis script, have you verified that it still produces the correct output?
