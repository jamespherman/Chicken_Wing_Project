6. KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. OUTLIER IMPACT: A single 22-second recording severely distorted the
   correlation structure. Always check for extreme outliers in small samples.

2. SPARSE SOLUTION: Only 5 of 18 oculometric features predict duration,
   suggesting most metrics are independent of recording length (desirable
   for skill assessment).

3. PUPIL METRICS DOMINATE: 3 of 5 selected features relate to pupil
   dynamics (raw_pupil_std, mean_residual, regression_r_squared).

4. ONLY ONE SIGNIFICANT CORRELATION: mean_residual (r=-0.58, p=0.037)
   is the only feature with p < 0.05. All others are exploratory.

5. MODERATE PREDICTION: R²=0.66 means ~66% of duration variance is
   explained by these 5 features, but with n=13 this is likely optimistic.

================================================================================
7. LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. SMALL SAMPLE SIZE: n=13 provides limited statistical power. These
   findings should be considered hypothesis-generating, not confirmatory.

2. OVERFITTING RISK: With 18 features and 13 subjects, even regularized
   models may overfit. Cross-validated R² was not stable.

3. NO CROSS-VALIDATION: Leave-one-out CV produced unstable estimates.
   More subjects needed for reliable out-of-sample performance.

4. CAUSALITY UNKNOWN: These are correlational findings. We cannot determine
   whether these metrics CAUSE sessions to be longer/shorter or simply
   CO-OCCUR with duration.

================================================================================
8. RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

1. Collect more subjects (target n≥30) before drawing firm conclusions.

2. Consider the 22-second recording - why was it so short? Equipment
   failure? Aborted procedure? This context matters for interpretation.

3. For skill assessment, the finding that most oculometric metrics are
   INDEPENDENT of duration is reassuring - they measure behavior, not
   just session length.

4. The mean_residual finding (p=0.037) warrants further investigation
   as a potential indicator of cognitive state affecting session duration.
